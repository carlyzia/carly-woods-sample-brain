# Process: Enquiry Qualification

## Trigger

A new website, phone or referral enquiry is received.

## Owner

Sales coordinator.

## Inputs required

Name, contact details, property suburb, ownership status, renovation type, indicative budget, desired timing and lead source.

## Steps

1. Check for missing required information.
2. Create or update the CRM contact.
3. Record the property and project details.
4. Apply the lead source.
5. Compare the enquiry with qualification criteria.
6. Prepare a summary for human review.
7. Send the approved next-step message.
8. Create the follow-up task.

## AI may

Identify missing fields, summarise the enquiry, draft a response and flag qualification concerns.

## A human must

Decide whether the project is accepted and confirm any budget or timing statement.

## Done when

The CRM is complete, the lead has an owner and stage, the next action is sent and follow-up is scheduled.
