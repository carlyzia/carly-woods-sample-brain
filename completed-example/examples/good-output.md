# Good Output Example

## Task

Summarise a new renovation enquiry for the sales coordinator.

## Good output

**Project:** Rear extension and kitchen renovation  
**Location:** Paddington, QLD  
**Timing:** Wants construction to start within six months  
**Budget:** $220,000 indicated  
**Known:** Owner-occupied property; concept sketches prepared  
**Missing:** Ownership confirmation, approval status and whether budget includes temporary accommodation  
**Fit flag:** Potential fit, but timing may be unrealistic until approval status is confirmed  
**Recommended next step:** Ask the three missing questions before offering a feasibility call

## Why this is good

It separates facts from missing information, avoids promising suitability and gives the human a clear next action.
