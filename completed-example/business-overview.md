# Business Overview

## Fictional company

**Harbour & Hammer Projects Pty Ltd**

Harbour & Hammer is a fictional residential renovation company in Brisbane. It employs 28 people across sales, estimating, project delivery, finance and administration.

## What we sell

Residential renovations valued between $150,000 and $900,000, including kitchens, extensions and whole-home renovations.

## Who we help

Homeowners who want one accountable team from initial feasibility through construction and handover.

## How work moves

1. Website or referral enquiry
2. Qualification call
3. Paid feasibility and site visit
4. Estimate and concept scope
5. Building agreement
6. Pre-construction approvals
7. Construction
8. Handover and defects period

## Current priorities

- reduce enquiry-to-feasibility admin
- shorten estimate turnaround
- stop project information being retyped
- improve client updates
- document recurring work before automating it

## Non-negotiables

Safety and compliance stay human-supervised. Pricing is reviewed before it leaves the business. No private client information goes into unapproved AI tools.
