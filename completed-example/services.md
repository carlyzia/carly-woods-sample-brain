# Services

## Renovation feasibility

A paid early-stage assessment covering the site, goals, likely constraints, initial scope and indicative investment range.

**Good fit:** the owner has a real property, realistic timeline and budget aligned to substantial renovation work.

**Not a fit:** free design requests, minor handyman jobs or requests for a binding quote without investigation.

## Design and pre-construction

Concept development, selections, consultant coordination, approvals, scope definition and construction pricing.

## Construction delivery

Project management, trades coordination, quality control, client communication, variations, handover and defects management.

## Decision rule

Never promise price, timing, approval or buildability without the appropriate human review and supporting project information.
