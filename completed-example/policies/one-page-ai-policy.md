# One-Page AI Policy

## 1. What tools may we use?

Only named tools approved by the business, using business-managed accounts with appropriate privacy settings.

## 2. What never goes into them?

Passwords, banking details, identity documents, private client information, confidential employee information or anything we are not authorised to share.

## 3. What needs a human check?

Anything client-facing, numerical, contractual, safety-related, compliance-related or used to make a meaningful decision about a person.

## 4. Who do we ask?

The operations manager. If unsure, stop and ask before uploading, connecting or sending.
