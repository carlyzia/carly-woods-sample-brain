# Brand Voice

## Voice

Calm, practical, direct and reassuring. We sound like an experienced project leader, not a lifestyle magazine or pushy salesperson.

## Writing rules

- use plain English
- lead with the answer
- keep paragraphs short
- explain construction terms
- state assumptions and next steps
- do not use fake urgency
- do not promise outcomes requiring inspection or approval

## Example

Weak: "Transform your dream into reality with our unparalleled renovation solutions."

Good: "We manage the renovation from feasibility to handover, so you know what happens next, who owns it and where decisions are needed."
