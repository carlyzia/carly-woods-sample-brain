# Customers

## Primary customer

A busy homeowner renovating their principal residence. They value clarity, accountability and a managed process more than the cheapest quote.

## Common concerns

- budget blowouts
- unclear timelines
- coordinating trades themselves
- expensive decisions with incomplete information
- poor communication during construction

## What earns trust

Clear next steps, honest boundaries, documented assumptions, prompt updates and plain-English explanations.

## What to avoid

Do not pressure, exaggerate certainty or describe every lead as a perfect fit. If information is missing, state what is needed before giving a reliable answer.
