# Start Here

Do not fill every file in one sitting. Build the minimum useful brain first.

## Your first 30 minutes

1. Copy this repository into a **private** repository.
2. Complete the business overview template.
3. Complete the services and brand voice templates.
4. Document one task you repeat every week.
5. Give your AI access only to the files required for the task.

## The five-pillar minimum

1. **Business:** what the company does and how it makes money.
2. **Customers:** who you serve, what they need and what they do not want.
3. **Work:** services, processes and decision rules.
4. **Voice:** how the business communicates, including examples.
5. **Boundaries:** security, approvals and tasks that always need a human.

When a process, offer, tool or rule changes, update the relevant file. A business brain that is six months wrong is worse than no brain at all.
